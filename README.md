## 1. Objective

Retailers must hold enough inventory to satisfy demand without tying up
capital in surplus stock. Project FORESIGHT provides an end-to-end capability:

    clean extracts -> profile demand -> forecast weekly demand ->
    score stockout / overstock risk -> prioritise rupee-based decisions

delivered through a planning dashboard and an executive readout.

## 2. Official data contract (expected — NOT yet present)

The four official extracts are expected under `data/raw/`. They are **input
locations only** — they must be provided by the internship and must **not**
be created, downloaded, or fabricated.

| Extract (file) | Official columns |
|---|---|
| `sales_daily.csv` | date, sku_id, units_sold, revenue, unit_price, promo_flag |
| `sku_master.csv` | sku_id, category, subcategory, launch_date, unit_cost, list_price |
| `calendar.csv` | date, week, month, season, is_holiday, promo_event |
| `inventory_snapshots.csv` | date, sku_id, on_hand_units, on_order_units, lead_time_days, reorder_point |

> **Warning:** the files currently under `data/raw/` are the legacy
> Mini-FORESIGHT demo extracts. They do **not** match the official schema
> above and are kept only as framework/reference. They are not official data.

## 6. Legacy Mini-FORESIGHT (important)

The pre-existing notebooks (`01_data_understanding` … `08_recommendations`)
and `app/app.py` implement an educational daily, 3-SKU demo. They are kept
intact for reference and **must not** be reported as official Project
FORESIGHT results. Their logic (daily horizon, `shift(1)` naive baseline,
single 70/30 split, reorder-only risk without rupee impact) is a learning
exercise and will be replaced by the official D3 / D4 logic.
